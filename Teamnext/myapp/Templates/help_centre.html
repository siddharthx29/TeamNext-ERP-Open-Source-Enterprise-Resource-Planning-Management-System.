Frequently Asked Questions
How do I contact TeamNext Support directly?
You can send an email directly to our support desk at main@teamnexterp.com, or use the interactive inquiry form on the right of this page. Our technical team responds to all incoming inquiries promptly.
How do I create and manage support tickets?
Navigate to the Tickets section from the left sidebar. There you can create new tickets, set priority levels, assign team members, and track ticket status in real-time.
How do I update company settings and member permissions?
Go to Settings to update company legal name, contact numbers, and industry settings. For user management, visit Users & Access.
What email delivery service does TeamNext use?
TeamNext ERP features an integrated Brevo HTTP API email dispatch engine with standard fallback support. All automated notifications, OTP verifications, and support inquiry alerts are processed seamlessly.
Where can I find financial and inventory reports?
Financial management, invoicing, and expenses are located in the Finance module. Comprehensive system analytics and reporting can be exported from the Reports page.
